5 Tips to Do It Correctly

  • Train with intention: Focus one mechanic per block instead of random free play.
  • Slow before fast: Start controlled reps, then add speed after clean execution.
  • Use replay checkpoints: Review goals against you and pause 5 seconds before each mistake.
  • Prioritize recoveries: Fast landings and boost pathing increase consistency instantly.
  • Measure weekly: Track shot accuracy, save rate, and boost management each week.
